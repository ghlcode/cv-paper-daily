## 1. Non-penetrating World Models

- **作者**：Yuhang Dai, Yinwei Wu, Ziyue Lin et al.
- **投稿**：2026-11-20 ｜ **分类**：cs.CV, cs.AI
- **备注**：20 pages, 13 figures. Code: https://github.com/yuhangdai427/NoPeWorld

We study a new problem of preventing object penetration in interactive video world models. To understand how existing models handle this, we introduce NoPeBench, a benchmark that evaluates how world models respond to physical obstacles. Our evaluation reveals that none of the open-source models can reliably prevent such failures. Surprisingly, however, these models already encode the information needed to predict such violations. We find that the hidden states of diffusion transformers (DiTs) can predict whether a candidate action will penetrate an obstacle. Building on this, we introduce NoPeWorld, a lightweight framework that detects and prevents penetration. Specifically, we train a small prediction head on top of a frozen DiT's hidden states to read out if a candidate action is safe or not. Unsafe actions are then replaced with safe ones that best preserve the original intent. Despite its simplicity, NoPeWorld substantially reduces the physical violation rate, averaged over six video world models, from 74% to 16%. Remarkably, our method shows zero-shot generalization to open-domain imagery, third-person viewpoints, and moving obstacles. We publicly release our code, the NoPeBench benchmark, and model weights. Project Page: https://nopeworld.github.io .

## 2. DiTraR: Diverse Scorers and Transcript-Guided Re-ranking for Video Corpus Moment Retrieval

- **作者**：Mohammad Alijanpour Shalmani, Alale Rezvani Boroujeni, Ali Amini
- **投稿**：2026-11-20 ｜ **分类**：cs.CV, cs.IR

Video corpus moment retrieval asks a system to find, in a large collection of untrimmed videos with subtitles, the video and the time interval that a natural-language query describes. We find that most top-ranked errors of a strong corpus-level scorer come from choosing the wrong video, not from imprecise boundaries, and that re-weighting the evidence the scorer already uses does not fix them. We present DiTraR, which addresses video selection from two complementary directions. A witness-interval scorer decomposes the query into probes and scores every candidate interval of every corpus video exactly; a multi-scorer energy ensemble averages the energies of independently trained scorers before one exhaustive search; and a transcript verifier, a frozen language model that reads the time-stamped dialogue of the highest-ranked videos, re-orders the moments without changing their boundaries. With all settings frozen on development data and a single evaluation, DiTraR reaches 24.36 and 13.55 R@1 at IoU 0.5 and 0.7 on the TVR validation split. The ensemble improves queries about visual content, the verifier improves queries about dialogue, and their gains largely add up.

## 3. ESSI: Computationally Efficient Semantic Search for Sparse Autoencoder Concepts

- **作者**：James Baker
- **投稿**：2026-11-20 ｜ **分类**：cs.CV

Sparse autoencoders (SAEs) decompose diffusion model activations into interpretable features, but steering a model with them requires identifying which features map to a given semantic concept, if any. Existing methods for this concept to feature task are expensive, relying on grid searches or repeated passes through the frozen UNet. We propose Efficient Semantic Sparse Identification (ESSI). Using segmentation masks to label SDXL activations as concept or non-concept, ESSI ranks SAE features and binary-searches for the smallest set on which a linear classifier still separates positive from negative samples while not falling below a performance threshold, then reuses that classifier to calibrate the steering strength. We evaluate the same selected features on both concept insertion and object unlearning. Compared with prior work, ESSI is as cheap or cheaper, and performs well on both insertion and removal.

## 4. Augmentation of cardiovascular flow measurements with generative diffusion models

- **作者**：Fabio Guglietta, Tianyi Li, Francesco Viola et al.
- **投稿**：2026-11-20 ｜ **分类**：physics.flu-dyn, cs.CV

Cardiovascular imaging offers several ways to measure blood flow, but each provides an incomplete, resolution-limited, or noisy view of the underlying dynamics. Reconstructing the missing information is inherently ambiguous because several flow fields may be compatible with the same observation. Here, we investigate generative diffusion models as tools for cardiovascular-flow generation and reconstruction using a fully controlled state-of-the-art dataset from a digital twin of a healthy human left heart. First, we show how unconditional samples reproduce the changing anatomy and the highly fluctuating statistical properties of the flow throughout the cardiac cycle. Second, we test the conditional inference of our generative model by reconstructing an entire 2D planar section of geometry and flow configurations from a limited portion. Repeated reconstructions allow us to represent a range of plausible completions, going beyond a single deterministic estimate, while their mean retains the principal features of the reference flow. Comparison with a Gaussian process regression baseline on the same reduced domain highlights the main advantage of diffusion models: their reconstructed ensembles capture a broader range of velocity and gradient fluctuations, better encompassing ground-truth features that the baseline distributions can miss. Our results provide a quantitative proof of concept for probabilistic reconstruction of incomplete cardiovascular-flow measurements and demonstrate the potential for groundbreaking applications to the augmentation of in-vivo cardiovascular imaging data.

## 5. StereoVLM: Binocular Vision-Language Models for Metric 3D Spatial Reasoning

- **作者**：Chi Yan, Guoyang Zhao, Wentao Yuan et al.
- **投稿**：2026-11-20 ｜ **分类**：cs.CV, cs.AI, cs.RO
- **备注**：Project page: https://xpeng-robotics.github.io/stereovlm/

Vision-language models (VLMs) exhibit strong semantic understanding but remain limited in their ability to reason about metric 3D space in the physical world. Existing VLMs typically infer 3D scene structure from one or more images without known camera calibration or cross-view geometric constraints. This makes metric 3D inference inherently ambiguous and leaves it unclear whether their spatial predictions are grounded in visual evidence or driven primarily by semantic priors. In contrast, calibrated stereo provides explicit cross-view geometry that resolves this ambiguity and offers a more reliable foundation for metric 3D perception and geometric generalization. We investigate how VLMs can learn transferable 3D representations directly from calibrated binocular observations. To this end, we introduce StereoVLM, the first geometry-aware binocular VLM for metric 3D spatial reasoning. StereoVLM follows a hierarchical learning paradigm spanning from cross-view correspondence and disparity estimation to calibration-conditioned metric depth perception and semantic spatial reasoning. We further find that disparity supervision generalizes substantially better than direct binocular depth supervision on unseen environments. To evaluate these capabilities, we introduce Stereo3D-Bench, a comprehensive benchmark across three levels, from point-level geometry to object-level spatial reasoning. StereoVLM-8B achieves 95.17% depth accuracy ($δ_1$) and a 66.98 average object-level score on Stereo3D-Bench. Intervention results further indicate that the model's performance on Stereo3D-Bench benefits from valid cross-view geometry and cannot be explained solely by additional visual tokens or semantic priors. Hierarchical geometric supervision also yields average gains of 3.54 points across five spatial benchmarks and 4.3 percentage points in RoboCasa manipulation success.

## 6. Star Panoptic Quality: Selectively Evaluating Split and Merged Instances

- **作者**：Erik Großkopf, Paula Tamara Buzduga, Hendrik Möller et al.
- **投稿**：2026-11-20 ｜ **分类**：cs.CV, cs.AI
- **备注**：9 pages, 7 figures

Detecting individual instances is crucial for many segmentation problems. However, expert instance annotations are often unavailable, so instances are approximated heuristically (e.g., as connected components of semantic maps), which can merge touching objects or split fragmented ones. Even when expert annotations exist, expert disagreement or structural delineation biases can yield split or merged reference instances, corresponding to over- or under-segmentation, respectively. Panoptic Quality (PQ) and related metrics capture both segmentation and detection quality while weighting all instances equally, regardless of size. Yet when the references are biased, these metrics can neither detect split or merged instances nor distinguish valid from invalid overor under-segmentation. We present Star Panoptic Quality (StarPQ), a novel evaluation ramework, which closes this gap in two steps: First, we construct a so-called star forest in the bipartite graph whose clean, merge, and split stars isolate different failure modes. Second, we calculate the final metric by individually weighting each failure mode, allowing practitioners to account for instance delineation biases. Across various experiments, we demonstrate that StarPQ not only enables targeted penalization or eniency towards specific failure modes, but also serves as a diagnostic tool to reveal their prevalence.

## 7. AnimalFarm: Auditing the Generation and Detection of Hateful Images Featuring Anthropomorphic Characters

## 8. QuantStream3D: Geometry-Aligned Low-Bit KV Cache Quantization for Streaming Visual Geometry Transformers

- **投稿**：2026-11-20 ｜ **分类**：cs.CV
- **备注**：19 pages, 5 figures. Includes appendix

Streaming visual geometry transformers retain historical key-value (KV) states as cross-view geometric memory, but growing caches constrain long-sequence inference. We systematically characterize KV quantization in streaming geometry and uncover two linked challenges: Nonuniform Geometric Sensitivity and Cache-Geometry Mismatch. Quantization sensitivity varies across precision choices and cache locations, while perturbed retrieval changes geometry-head inputs and propagates through subsequent predictions and KV writes. Guided by these findings, we propose QuantStream3D, to our knowledge the first dedicated KV quantization framework for streaming visual geometry transformers. Integration with existing native cache-pruning methods further pushes compression while improving reconstruction quality on NeuralRGBD. Our optimized StreamVGGT backend achieves $5.05\times$ physical KV compression and 23.1% lower latency at batch size one. Under a fixed memory budget on the same GPU, it supports $5.4\times$ the maximum batch size and up to $1.67\times$ the peak throughput of unquantized inference. Anchor tokens let a vision transformer exchange global information through a few summaries, but nothing forces different anchors to summarise different things. ## 16. Redesigning Neural Networks to Not be Confidently Wrong even to Strong Adversarial Attacks

- **备注**：Code: https://codeberg.org/mwspratling/STEAL/

Early deep learning models attempted to simulate visual perception using a neural hierarchy that alternated between two types of layer: one modelled on cortical simple cells (that was designed to increase selectivity), and one modelled on cortical complex cells (that aimed to increase tolerance). ## 18. $\varepsilon$-SegFormer: Multi-Resolution Vision Transformers for Sparsely Supervised Segmentation in Electron Microscopy

- **作者**：Erfan Amidi, Sheida RahnamaiKordasiabi, Edoardo Giacomello et al.
- **投稿**：2026-11-20 ｜ **分类**：cs.CV, cs.LG
- **备注**：23 pages including supplementary material

Segmenting organelles in electron microscopy (EM) volumes requires dense annotations that take experts months to produce, and every gain in imaging resolution adds to this cost. Sparsely supervised methods reduce this cost to a small fraction of labeled pixels, but the convolutional networks they build on see a limited neighborhood around each pixel, while the identity of an organelle often depends on context far beyond it. We introduce $\varepsilon$-SegFormer, a multi-resolution Vision Transformer for sparsely supervised semantic segmentation, which builds on the architecture of MuViT and extends the training method of $\varepsilon$-Seg. For each labeled pixel, $\varepsilon$-SegFormer samples views at three physical resolutions centered on it, embeds their patches into a single token sequence, and encodes every token's position in image coordinates, so that attention relates all views in one shared frame. A segmentation head classifies the pixel from the tokens around it; during training, the pixel's own token is hidden, so the label must be predicted from context rather than local appearance. The model is trained in a single stage, without pretraining, with an auxiliary reconstruction loss. On the BetaSeg FIB-SEM dataset, $\varepsilon$-SegFormer learns from about 10,000 labeled pixels among the 670 million voxels inside three cells, and, given the cell mask, segments nuclei, mitochondria and insulin granules in a slice of a fourth, unseen cell with an average Dice of 0.89 (0.91 after post-processing). Ablations show that coarser views improve accuracy and that a compact head on local tokens works best, with the wider context entering through attention in the encoder.
